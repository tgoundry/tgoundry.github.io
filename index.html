Personal Mail Copy

Personal Mail Copy is a personal, single-user tool. It makes a one-way copy of the owner's own email into the owner's own Gmail account, so the mail can also be read in Gmail.

What it does

A small script, run on a computer the owner controls, reads recent messages from the owner's own mailbox and inserts a copy of each into the owner's Gmail account under a label. It never changes, moves or deletes mail in either place, and it never sends email.

What access it asks for

The only Google permission it requests is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.insert. That permission can add messages to the account. It cannot read, search, change or delete anything in Gmail.

Who can use it

Only the owner. This is not a public application, it has no other users, and nobody else is asked to sign in.

Your data

The tool does not collect, sell or share anyone's data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
